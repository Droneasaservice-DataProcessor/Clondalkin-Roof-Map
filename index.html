Apparent surface temperatures at emissivity 0.95, captured around midday. Suitable for tiles, membrane and concrete; not reliable on metal or glass (flashings, fans, rooflights). Accuracy approx. ±2 °C, so best used to compare nearby spots. Sun-facing slopes read warmer because of the sun, not defects. White areas: no thermal coverage.
